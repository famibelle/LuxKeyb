You speak it. Now you write it.

Lëtzebuergesch Clavier is a free, ad-free Android keyboard made for one language only: Lëtzebuergesch.

🛠️ Is your Luxembourgish a little rusty?
😤 Does your phone underline every word you type in red?
🤔 Do you hesitate over the spelling in every message?
➡️ This keyboard is made for you.

⌨️ ACCENTS RIGHT UNDER YOUR THUMB

é, ä, ë, ö and ü each have their own key, right on the keyboard. No more hunting for ë in a hidden menu. The others (è, à, ô, ê) are a long press away.

🧠 IT SUGGESTS THE WORDS

Type "Schueb" and it offers "Schueberfouer". After a space, it guesses the next word. The words you use most move up on their own.

🎙️ WHEN TYPING TAKES TOO LONG, DICTATE

Tap the microphone and speak: the text appears as you talk, with punctuation and capital letters. Recognition is provided by LuxASR, from the University of Luxembourg. An internet connection is needed.

✍️ IT FORGIVES TYPOS

A missing letter, a neighbouring key: the right word still comes up. Type "letzebuergesch" without accents and it offers "lëtzebuergesch".

✅ NO MORE RED EVERYWHERE

Once switched on in Android's settings, it stops underlining your Luxembourgish words in red, in WhatsApp, text messages or emails. Your French words too.

🇫🇷 A FRENCH WORD IN THE MIDDLE?

"ech hunn eng réunion muer" is written without switching keyboards. If you like, a second row also suggests French words.

📖 A DICTIONARY IN YOUR POCKET

The Wierderbuch translates Luxembourgish words into English, French, German or Portuguese, and the other way round, with example sentences from the official dictionary. Even offline.

🎮 LEARN WHILE YOU PLAY

Every word you write raises your level, from Ufänker 🌍 to Sproochenmeeschter 🧙. Seven games have you find, write or place words: Wuertsich, Wuertmix, Wuertriet, Wuertlück, Zuelwuert, Kräizwuert, Wuertplaz.

🎴 A COLLECTION SO YOU DON'T FORGET

Every word you win becomes a card to flip, with its meaning and an example. The collection brings them back for review at the right time, until they stick.

🌍 IN YOUR LANGUAGE

The app speaks English, French, German, Portuguese or Luxembourgish. Pick yours at the bottom of the Keyboard settings, even if your phone is set to another one.

🔒 YOUR WORDS STAY WITH YOU

• What you type never leaves your phone
• Dictation sends your voice to the University of Luxembourg, only when you tap the microphone and after you agree; it is transcribed, not kept
• No account, no ads
• Your passwords, names and numbers are never recorded
• Open source code, available for anyone to read

📱 EVERYWHERE ON YOUR PHONE

• WhatsApp, text messages, email, social media: it works in every app
• Android 7.0 and later, light or dark theme
• Guided setup in three steps, with a practice keyboard
• Android shows a warning when you switch it on: it does so for every keyboard, this is normal, the app's guide explains it

🙋 WHAT IT DOES NOT DO YET

No typing by sliding your finger from letter to letter. Dictation does not work offline.

💬 A WORD MISSING?

Let us know: github.com/famibelle/LuxKeyb. The dictionary grows with every version.
