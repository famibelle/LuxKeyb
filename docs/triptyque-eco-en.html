Free · No ads · No account · Voice typing
Lëtzebuergesch Clavier is a citizen project published by Médhi Famibelle. No personal data is collected: what you type stays on the device. Only voice typing, when you tap the mic, sends your voice to the University of Luxembourg's LuxASR service, which transcribes it without keeping it.

Why

Why a Luxembourgish keyboard?

  • Your phone “corrects” your Luxembourgish into German or French.
  • ë and ä hide in an accent menu, word after word.
  • You doubt your spelling in every message.
  • You can say it, but not write it.
  • This keyboard is made for you.

Let's go

Setup in 4 steps

  1. Open the Google Play page, it's free
    Scan this codePoint your camera at it: the Google Play page opens, all that's left is to tap Install
  2. Install the appFrom Google Play, like any other app: installation and updates are automatic.
  3. Turn the keyboard on in settingsA button in the app goes straight to the right Android screen. The system then shows a warning about typing: it is generic, Android shows it for every third-party keyboard, and this one sends nothing you type.
  4. Pick it and writeThe keyboard picker opens: choose Lëtzebuergesch Clavier and type your first word.
Features

What it does for you

  • The Luxembourgish word appears before you finish typing it
  • é, ä, ë have their own keys; for other accents, hold your finger down
  • It understands you without accents too: “letzebuergesch” suggests “lëtzebuergesch”
  • Your Luxembourgish words are no longer underlined in red, in any app
  • Tap the mic and speak: it writes in Luxembourgish (internet connection needed)
It also guesses the next word: after “op der”, it already suggests “Stad”, “Police”, “Rue”. Fewer letters to type, fewer mistakes.
And for learning

Learn as you type

🎮 Seven games word search, anagrams, a word in six tries, fill-in-the-blanks, numbers, crosswords, word fill-ins.
🃏 A card deck every word you win becomes a card, and the words you type move it forward.
📖 An EN ↔ LB dictionary a word escapes you? It translates both ways, offline.
