Writing Luxembourgish just got even easier
In one month, the keyboard has learned to listen to you, to catch your typos and to make full use of a tablet. Here is what changes for you.
Voice dictation
Speak, and the text writes itself

Tap the microphone to the right of the suggestion bar and speak Luxembourgish. The text appears as you talk, with punctuation and capitals, and the microphone closes by itself when you stop speaking.

Spoken, not typed.

Recognition is provided by LuxASR, the University of Luxembourg's service. Before your first dictation, a screen explains where your voice goes; nothing is sent until you agree. What you type stays on your phone.

 
Typos
The keyboard catches your hurried fingers

A slipping thumb, two swapped letters, a forgotten space: the bar now suggests the right word.

echhunn→ech hunn
mioen→Moien
haud→Haus haut
Kafi→Kaffi
Typed “echhunn geschter an der schiul gescahfft”, fixed with one tap per mistake.
  • Two words typed together are split apart, without breaking compound words like “Schoulbus”.
  • The neighbouring key is recognised: “haud” gives “Haus” first, because s sits next to d.
  • A double letter typed only once is found again: “ales” gives “alles”.
 
Cards to discover Luxembourgish: every word you win while playing becomes a card, with its meaning, a sentence from the official dictionary and a landscape of the country. The rarer the word, the nobler the metal, from pewter to gold.
The notebook
Cards to collect and to give away

Your cards go into a notebook, which has become a real album.

  • Series to complete: numbers, drawings, games, landscapes. An empty slot gives a hint, never the answer.
  • Give a card away, hand to hand: the two phones side by side, a code to scan, and the card moves from one notebook to the other. It leaves yours, like a real card you hand over.
  • Share a card as a picture, with the link to install the keyboard.
  • A seal on the words you have learned, and a small quill on those you typed yourself in a real message.
 
Tablets
A keyboard sized for tablets
  • Taller keys, and in landscape a keyboard split in two, within reach of your thumbs.
  • With a Bluetooth keyboard or a keyboard case, only the suggestion bar stays on screen.
  • The grid games and the dictionary open side by side: the grid on one side, the words on the other.
  • Rotating the screen no longer loses your game.
On a tablet in landscape, the keyboard is shared between both thumbs.
 
Zuelwuert
Every number from 0 to 100

The numbers game now covers the whole hundred. In Easy, it shows the number alone; after that, a multiplication or an addition. You learn to write “sechsafofzeg” and “sechsandrësseg”, and why one of them loses its n.

 
Setup
Ready in fewer steps

The activation button opens the right Android screen directly, the app tells you how to answer Android's warning, and the first word you type is enough to confirm everything.

And a treat for the eyes

Each card opens a window onto a place in the country, from the Rout Bréck to the Musel. It remembers the season you won it in: snow in winter, leaves in autumn, fireworks on National Day. Tilt the phone: the landscape gains depth and light runs across the frame, from the pewter of common cards to the gold of very rare ones.

A card won on National Day keeps its bunting, an autumn card its russet leaves.
You speak it. Now you write it.
